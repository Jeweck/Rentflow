Everything you need to manage rentals in one place.

RentFlow is a rental management system designed to simplify the process of managing rental properties. It provides landlords with a convenient way to manage tenants, monitor billing, track payments, and organize rental information, while giving tenants access to their bills and payment submissions.

Built for the way rentals actually run.

Each part of RentFlow covers an everyday task, from keeping records to checking receipts.

Tenant Management

Keep tenant information organized and easily accessible.

Billing Management

Create, manage, and monitor tenant bills and payment due dates.

Payment Tracking

Track submitted payment receipts and monitor payment statuses.

Rental Property Management

Organize rental rooms and keep property details in one place.

Payment Overview

Review recent payments, pending receipts, and rental income records.

Rental Assistance

Get guidance on using the system and navigating rental management features.

How it works

Access Your Account

Log in to your account and access features based on your role as a landlord or tenant.

Manage Your Rental Activities

Landlords can manage tenants, rooms, and billing, while tenants can view their bills and payment details.

Submit and Monitor Payments

Tenants can submit payment receipts for verification, while landlords review and update payment statuses.
